# Play Store listing — HexaDominate

## Title (max 30)

## Short description (max 80)
Multiply, jump and convert your rivals on a hex board. 300 offline levels.

## Full description (max 4000)
Take over the board one hex at a time.

HexaDominate is a hexagonal strategy board game with simple rules and a lot of
room to think. Multiply a token into a neighbouring hex to grow, or jump two
hexes to reposition — and every enemy token beside the hex you land on switches
to your colour. When the board fills up, whoever holds the most hexes wins.

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Works fully offline — no account, no sign-in, no sign-up
• Your progress, settings and statistics stay on your device
• Every move is saved, so you can close the app mid-game and continue later

300 LEVELS
• Six tiers from Tutorial to Master, each with new board shapes and opponents
• Hexagons, rings, stars, triangles and boards riddled with holes
• Earn up to three stars per level: win, win big, win big without undo

OPPONENTS THAT ACTUALLY THINK
• Four difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard and Expert
• The harder ones search several moves ahead and punish loose play
• Face up to three opponents at once and let them fight each other

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Local 2-player on one device, with an optional pass-the-device screen
• Quick Game: pick a board and opponents, or hit Surprise Me
• 30 challenge boards, including outnumbered starts and four-way battles
• A daily challenge — the same board for everyone, generated from the date

MAKE IT YOURS
• 10 token symbols to unlock and 6 colours
• Dark, Light, Midnight and OLED themes, plus three board styles
• Sound, music and haptics, all switchable
• High contrast mode, and symbols on every token so colour is never the only
  way to tell players apart

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Statistics: games, win rate, captures, streaks and more
• 12 achievements
• Export and import a backup file whenever you like

No account. No servers. Just the board.
